Fungsi Bagian Tubuh (What can we do?)

  • eyes → to see (melihat)
  • ears → to hear (mendengar)
  • nose → to smell (mencium bau)
  • mouth → to eat and to speak (makan dan berbicara)
  • hands → to hold, to take, to write (memegang, mengambil, menulis)
  • legs → to walk and to run (berjalan dan berlari)
  • feet → to walk (berjalan)

Contoh Kalimat

  • “I have two eyes.”
  • “She has long black hair.”
  • “My nose is small.”
  • “He has strong legs.”

Kapan Dipakai?

  • Hot day (hari panas) → T-shirt, shorts, dress
  • Rainy day (hari hujan) → raincoat, boots, umbrella (payung)
  • Cold day (hari dingin) → sweater, jacket, scarf, gloves

Contoh Kalimat

  • “I wear a red jacket.”
  • “She wears a yellow dress.”
  • “He is wearing a hat.”
  • “What are you wearing today?” — “I am wearing a blue T-shirt.”

3. Tips Menyusun Kata

Kalimat bahasa Inggris umumnya berurutan: Subject + Verb + Object (Subjek + Kata Kerja + Objek).

Contoh: I (subject) + have (verb) + two eyes (object) → “I have two eyes.”

Saat menyusun kata acak, cari dulu subjeknya, lalu kata kerjanya, kemudian objeknya.

4. Tips Menerjemahkan

  • Perhatikan subjek he (dia laki-laki) dan she (dia perempuan).
  • Kata kerja untuk orang ketiga tunggal (he/she) ditambah -s: “She wears a dress.”, “He has strong legs.”
  • Kata sifat diletakkan sebelum kata benda: red shirt (kemeja merah), bukan shirt red.
