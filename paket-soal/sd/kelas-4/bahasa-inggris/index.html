Bab 1 • Unit 3 – Welcome To My House

Ruangan dan benda di rumah, serta preposisi tempat

  • Rooms (ruangan): living room (ruang tamu), kitchen (dapur), bathroom (kamar mandi), bedroom (kamar tidur), dining room (ruang makan), garage (garasi).My house has four rooms: a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom, and a bedroom.
  • In the living room: sofa, television, clock (on the wall), lamp.There is a sofa and a television in the living room.
  • In the kitchen: stove, refrigerator.Mother cooks rice on the stove.
  • In the bathroom: mirror, towel, toothbrush.I take a bath in the bathroom.
  • In the bedroom: bed, blanket, pillow, wardrobe.The wardrobe is in the bedroom.
  • In the dining room: dining table, plate, spoon, fork, knife, glass.We eat breakfast in the dining room.
  • In the garage: car, bicycle, motorcycle.Father parks the car in the garage.
  • Prepositions (kata depan tempat): in (di dalam), on (di atas), under (di bawah), beside (di samping), behind (di belakang), in front of (di depan).The book is on the table. The ball is under the table. The lamp is beside the bed.
  • Where is …? (Di mana …?) — jawab dengan preposisi + tempat.Where is the wardrobe? It is in the bedroom.
  • What is …? (Apa …?) — untuk menanyakan benda.What is on the table? It is a plate.
  • There is … untuk satu benda; There are … untuk lebih dari satu.There is a mirror in the bathroom. There are four rooms in my house.
  • Benda dan kegunaannya: towel = handuk (mengeringkan badan), glass = gelas (untuk minum), spoon = sendok (untuk makan), toothbrush = sikat gigi.After taking a bath, I dry my body with a towel.

Bab 2 • Unit 4 – What Can You Do At Home?

Kata can dan can't untuk kegiatan di rumah

  • can = bisa / dapat; can't = cannot = tidak bisa.I can cook. I can't swim in the bathroom.
  • Rumus: can / can't + kata kerja bentuk 1.can cook, can read, can sleep, can watch TV, can take a bath.
  • Can you …? (Bisakah kamu …?) — jawab: Yes, I can. / No, I can't.Can you sing a song in the living room? Yes, I can.
  • Activities in the bedroom: sleep, read a book.I can read a book in the bedroom.
  • Activities in the kitchen: cook.Mother can cook fried rice in the kitchen.
  • Activities in the bathroom: take a bath (bukan swim!).I can take a bath in the bathroom.
  • Activities in the living room: watch TV, read a book, sing a song.We can watch TV in the living room.
  • Activities in the dining room: eat breakfast, lunch, dinner.I eat breakfast with my family in the dining room.
  • Activities in the garage: park the car, wash the car.Father washes the car in the garage.
  • can't untuk hal yang tidak mungkin: tidak bisa berenang di kamar mandi, tidak bisa memasak di kamar tidur.I can't swim in the bathroom. I can't cook in the bedroom.
  • Menyusun kalimat: Subjek + can/can't + kata kerja + keterangan tempat.Mother cooks in the kitchen. → Mother can cook in the kitchen.
  • Kata tanya tempat: Where (di mana) dipakai bila jawabannya berupa tempat.Where do you live? I live in Ungaran.

📝 Simulasi ASAS

Petunjuk Pengerjaan

  • Jumlah soal 40: PG No. 1–20, PG Kompleks No. 21–25, Benar/Salah No. 26–30, Isian No. 31–35, Uraian No. 36–40.
  • Waktu pengerjaan 90 menit. Jika waktu habis, simulasi otomatis selesai.
  • Gunakan palet nomor untuk berpindah soal. Hijau = sudah dijawab, kuning = ragu-ragu.
  • Tombol Ragu-ragu menandai soal yang belum yakin jawabannya.
  • PG, PG Kompleks, Benar/Salah, dan Isian dinilai otomatis. Uraian dinilai olehmu sendiri (0–3) di layar hasil dengan membandingkan jawabanmu dengan jawaban model.
  • Total skor maksimal 65. Nilai = total skor ÷ 65 × 100. PG Kompleks bernilai 2 poin bila semua pilihan benar; setiap soal Benar/Salah bernilai 2 poin.
