Bab 1 • Unit 3 – Hobbies

Mengenal hobi, ungkapan kesukaan, dan teks bacaan tentang hobi

  • Hobby (hobi) adalah kegiatan yang kita suka lakukan di waktu luang.reading, swimming, cooking, fishing, dancing, singing, drawing, cycling, playing football.
  • Ungkapan suka: I like … / She likes … / He likes … — kata kerja setelah like/likes memakai -ing.I like reading. / Sinta likes dancing.
  • Ungkapan tidak suka: I don't like … / She doesn't like … / They hate …I don't like swimming. / She doesn't like running.
  • He / she / it memakai likes dan doesn't; I / you / we / they memakai like dan don't.He likes reading. / They like reading.
  • Kata kerja + -ing: read → reading, swim → swimming, cook → cooking, dance → dancing, sing → singing, play → playing.My brother likes swimming.
  • Menyusun kalimat: Subjek + like/likes + kata kerja -ing (+ keterangan).Andi likes playing football.
  • Perlengkapan hobi: fishing rod (memancing), ball (sepak bola), book (membaca), bicycle (bersepeda), kite (layang-layang).A fishing rod is used for fishing.
  • Membaca teks tentang hobi: perhatikan siapa (who), kegiatannya apa (what), kapan (when), dan di mana (where).Budi swims every Saturday morning in the swimming pool.

Bab 2 • Unit 4 – Days and Months of the Week

Nama hari dan bulan, urutannya, serta hari peringatan

  • Nama-nama hari (7 hari): Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.Today is Monday.
  • Urutan hari: sesudah Monday adalah Tuesday; sebelum Friday adalah Thursday.The day after Wednesday is Thursday.
  • Yesterday = kemarin, today = hari ini, tomorrow = besok.Yesterday was Monday. Today is Tuesday. Tomorrow is Wednesday.
  • Nama-nama bulan (12 bulan): January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.The first month of the year is January.
  • Urutan bulan: sesudah March adalah April; sebelum July adalah June.January, February, March, April.
  • Hari peringatan: Independence Day – 17 August; Kartini Day – 21 April; Pancasila Day – 1 June; Youth Pledge Day – 28 October.Indonesia's Independence Day is on 17 August.
  • Menyusun kalimat tentang hari: Subjek + is + nama hari.Today is Monday. / Tomorrow is Saturday.
  • Menyebut tanggal: tanggal + bulan, misalnya 21 April.Kartini Day is on 21 April.

📝 Simulasi ASAS

Petunjuk Pengerjaan

  • Jumlah soal 35: PG No. 1–12 & 21–29, PG Kompleks No. 13–14, Benar/Salah No. 15–16, Isian No. 17–18 & 30–32, Uraian No. 19–20 & 33–35.
  • Waktu pengerjaan 90 menit. Jika waktu habis, simulasi otomatis selesai.
  • Gunakan palet nomor untuk berpindah soal. Hijau = sudah dijawab, kuning = ragu-ragu.
  • Tombol Ragu-ragu menandai soal yang belum yakin jawabannya.
  • PG, PG Kompleks, Benar/Salah, dan Isian dinilai otomatis. Uraian dinilai olehmu sendiri (0–3) di layar hasil dengan membandingkan jawabanmu dengan jawaban model.
  • Total skor maksimal 52. Nilai = total skor ÷ 52 × 100. PG Kompleks bernilai 2 poin bila semua pilihan benar.
